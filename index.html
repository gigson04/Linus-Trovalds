ABOUT LINUS TORVALDS

Linus Torvalds (born December 28, 1969) is a Finnish-American software engineer and the creator of the Linux kernel. He is the principal developer of the Linux operating system and has led its development since 1991. Torvalds is also the creator of the Git distributed version control system and the maintainer of the Linux kernel.

Torvalds was born in Helsinki, Finland, and studied computer science at the University of Helsinki. He began developing the Linux kernel in 1991 as a hobby project while he was a student. The kernel was initially released under the GNU General Public License (GPL), which allows it to be freely distributed and modified.

Since its inception, the Linux kernel has become one of the most widely used operating system kernels in the world, powering everything from smartphones to supercomputers. Torvalds continues to lead the development of the Linux kernel and is considered one of the most influential figures in the open-source software movement.
